Personal History

  • Martha Pfeiffer: A Personal Connection
    January 5, 2025

    Martha Pfeiffer was my great-aunt, and her death in the Eastland Disaster has shaped my family’s understanding of tragedy and commemoration for generations. This episode explores what we know about Martha’s life and her connection to Western Electric. Early Life Martha was born in Chicago in 1897 to German immigrant parents. By 1915, she was working at Western Electric, the company that had chartered the Eastland for its annual employee picnic.
