Martha Pfeiffer: A Personal Connection

Published: January 5, 2025 | Episode 1

Martha Pfeiffer was my great-aunt, and her death in the Eastland Disaster has shaped my family’s understanding of tragedy and commemoration for generations. This episode explores what we know about Martha’s life and her connection to Western Electric.

Early Life

Martha was born in Chicago in 1897 to German immigrant parents. By 1915, she was working at Western Electric, the company that had chartered the Eastland for its annual employee picnic.

July 24, 1915

On that Saturday morning, Martha joined thousands of Western Electric employees and their families at the Chicago River dock. The Eastland was scheduled to take them across Lake Michigan to Michigan City, Indiana, for the company picnic.

Research Methodology

This episode demonstrates the forensic genealogy approach used throughout this project: starting with family knowledge, verifying through multiple contemporary sources, and building connections through archival research.
