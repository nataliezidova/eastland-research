Welcome to Flower in the River

This site documents the individuals connected to the 1915 Eastland Disaster in Chicago. Through systematic research and forensic genealogy, we’re uncovering the stories of those who were lost and their families.

About This Project

The Eastland Disaster occurred on July 24, 1915, when the SS Eastland capsized in the Chicago River, resulting in one of the deadliest maritime disasters in American history. This project combines genealogical research, archival investigation, and participatory storytelling to document each individual connected to this tragedy.

Every claim on this site is fully cited and sourced. We believe in letting the dead speak for themselves through evidence-based research.
