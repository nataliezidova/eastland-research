Thomas Marin's story illustrates how systematic archival research can uncover victims who have been missing from official counts for over a century.

## The Discovery

While reviewing National Archives records related to Eastland claims, I discovered documentation for Thomas Marin—a victim who does not appear in any of the established digital databases or death lists that have been maintained for decades.

## Why This Matters

The commercial "844" death toll that has been branded and marketed by heritage organizations is demonstrably incomplete. Thomas Marin is proof that the actual number of victims remains unknown, and that proper archival research continues to reveal new information.

## The Forensic Feedback Loop

This discovery exemplifies what I call the "Forensic Feedback Loop"—a methodology combining forensic genealogy, digital archaeology, and systematic archival research. By approaching the Eastland Disaster as an ongoing investigation rather than a closed historical event, we continue to find individuals who have been overlooked.
